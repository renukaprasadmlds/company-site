Three migration paths

MII and ME landscapes are rarely identical. The right target depends on how standard versus custom your existing application is — discovered in fit-gap, not assumed up front.

  • Mostly standard

    Fully to SAP DM

    When ME/MII is used mainly as MES/MOM execution — orders, WIP, routing, quality, operator UIs — map to SAP Digital Manufacturing as the cloud MES successor (SAP Customer Evolution Kit path).

    Best fit for plants that can adopt standard DM capabilities with configuration rather than rewriting plant-specific code.

  • Heavily custom

    Fully to SAP BTP

    When MII was primarily a custom application platform — plant-specific UIs, bespoke transactions, complex composition logic that does not map to DM standards — rebuild on SAP BTP (CAP, Integration Suite, Event Mesh, UI5) instead of forcing a square peg into DM.

    Best fit when the value is in your custom apps, not in standard MES features.

  • Mixed landscape

    DM and BTP together

    The most common real-world outcome: standard execution on DM, extensions and heavy custom logic on BTP, with phased coexistence until each plant is ready.

    Fit-gap splits the footprint — what becomes DM configuration, what becomes BTP services, and what stays temporarily on legacy MII/ME.

We start with Discover: inventory standard vs custom, score fit to DM, and only then recommend DM-only, BTP-only, or hybrid — so you do not over-buy MES or under-invest in extensions.

Phased coexistence

We do not recommend a risky big-bang cutover. Legacy and target systems run in parallel until the pilot line proves the model — whether the target is DM, BTP apps, or both.

FAQ

Why migrate from SAP MII or ME now?

ME and MII are retiring, with 15.5 as the final release. SAP provides a Customer Evolution Kit toward Digital Manufacturing. Moving on your schedule is safer than waiting for support cliffs.

Do we always move everything to SAP DM?

No. Mostly standard MES footprints usually go fully to DM. Heavily custom MII applications often rebuild on BTP. Mixed landscapes commonly use both — DM for standard execution, BTP for extensions — decided by fit-gap.

Can we migrate without stopping production?

Yes. Phased coexistence lets legacy and the chosen target (DM, BTP, or both) run together while you prove one line, then scale.

What about shop-floor connectivity?

We design software-defined edge layers (OPC UA, MQTT, and related patterns) so you are not forced into a hardware refresh.

How do we start with Dhiphos?

Discover (fit-gap on standard vs custom) → Prove (bounded PoC on the chosen path) → Scale (embedded or SOW). Email us to begin.
