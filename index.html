The migration window is open

SAP Manufacturing Execution and MII are retiring. Version 15.5 is the final release. Manufacturing IT leaders now face a scheduled decision: move on your timeline — or under end-of-support pressure.

The right landing zone is not always “everything to DM.” Depending on how standard or custom your existing apps are, the path may be fully to SAP DM, fully to SAP BTP, or both.
